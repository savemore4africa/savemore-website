Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: September 2026

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often. If you don't see what you're looking for, reach out to us on WhatsApp — the button is in the corner of this page.

Getting Started

How do I join SAVEMORE?
Visit the Member Portal and tap "New member? Create an account." You'll be asked for your personal details, KYC information (like your NIN and bank details), and to choose a savings product. See our KYC page for the full list of requirements.
What is the Application/Commitment Fee?
SAVEMORE charges a progressive Commitment Fee based on the total number of applicants at the time you join — it increases in tiers as SAVEMORE grows. Paying this fee also entitles you to 1 MORE participation unit. See our Fees & Charges page for the exact current figures.
Is SAVEMORE a bank?
No. SAVEMORE is a savings and financial support platform operated under Moresales Venture. It is not a licensed bank, microfinance institution, or regulated securities issuer. Please read our Terms & Conditions and Risk Disclosure for full details on what SAVEMORE is and isn't.

Savings Products

What savings products does SAVEMORE offer?
Four products: the Investment Savings Account, Emergency Savings Account, In-Dollar Savings Account, and the 100 Days Savings Challenge. Each has different minimum contributions and lock-up periods — see the Products section on our homepage.
How does the In-Dollar Savings Account work?
Your deposit is used to acquire USDT at the applicable rate at the time you deposit. At withdrawal, that USDT is sold at the applicable rate then. This means the naira value you get back can differ from what you put in, depending on exchange-rate movement. Full explanation on our How Your Money Is Handled page.
How do I make a deposit?
Log into your Member Portal and tap "Save Money." You'll see our bank details for transfer. After paying, enter the product and amount and tap "I've Made Payment" — SAVEMORE is notified automatically and will confirm your deposit.
How do I request a withdrawal?
On your Member Portal dashboard, tap "Withdraw," choose the product and amount, and submit. SAVEMORE reviews withdrawal requests according to the applicable terms of your chosen product (including any lock-up period).

MORE

What is MORE?
MORE is SAVEMORE's proprietary participation unit — not a currency. Its reference value is calculated using SAVEMORE's formula and can change as membership grows. Full details on our MORE page and in the MORE 1.0 document.
How do I sell my MORE back to SAVEMORE?
On your dashboard, under "MORE Participation," enter the number of units you'd like to sell and submit a request. SAVEMORE reviews these according to its sell-back rules and prevailing MORE reference value.

Account & Security

I forgot my password. What do I do?
On the Member Portal login screen, tap "Forgot Password?", enter your email, and you'll receive a reset link. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.
Is my information safe?
Your KYC details (like NIN and bank account number) are masked by default on your dashboard and only visible to you and authorized SAVEMORE administrators. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how your information is handled.
What is the PIN used for on withdrawals and sell-backs?
As an extra layer of protection, SAVEMORE requires a separate 4–6 digit PIN (different from your login password) before processing withdrawal or sell-back requests. You'll be asked to set this up the first time you use either feature.
