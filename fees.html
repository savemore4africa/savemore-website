Fees & Charges

Last updated: September 2026

This page sets out SAVEMORE's current fees and charges clearly, using the figures already established in SAVEMORE's governing documents. Where a fee is not yet defined for a specific situation, this is stated plainly rather than estimated.

Application/Renewal (Commitment) Fee

SAVEMORE applies a progressive Commitment Fee based on the total number of applicants/members at the time of application or renewal. Paying this fee also entitles the applicant to one (1) MORE participation unit, regardless of the fee amount paid at that tier.

Total Applicants (n)Commitment FeeMORE Received

41 – 80₦2,0001 MORE
81 – 120₦3,0001 MORE

The fee amount depends on how many total applicants SAVEMORE has at the time you apply or renew — it is not a flat fee that applies to everyone at all times. The same progression continues in further blocks of 40 applicants, with the fee increasing by ₦1,000 per block.
