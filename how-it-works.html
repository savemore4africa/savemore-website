How SAVEMORE Handles Your Savings

Last updated: September 2026

We believe members should clearly understand what happens to their money from the moment they join SAVEMORE. Here is the process, step by step.

The Savings Process

  1. You create a SAVEMORE member account through the Member Portal.
  2. You complete the applicable KYC requirements, including identification and next of kin details.
  3. You select a savings product: Investment Savings, Emergency Savings, In-Dollar Savings, or the 100 Days Savings Challenge.
  4. You make a contribution/deposit according to the terms of your chosen product.
  5. SAVEMORE records the transaction, including the date, amount, and product.
  6. Your savings balance is updated and reflected on your Member Portal dashboard.
  7. You can monitor your account at any time and request a withdrawal according to the applicable product terms.

How the In-Dollar Savings Account Works

This section explains a mechanism that involves currency exchange-rate risk. Please read it carefully before choosing this product.
  • When you deposit into the In-Dollar Savings Account, SAVEMORE uses that deposit to acquire USDT (a US-dollar-pegged digital asset) at the applicable rate at the time of your deposit.
  • Your savings are then held in this USDT-equivalent form.
  • When you request a withdrawal, the USDT is sold at the applicable rate at that time.
  • Because exchange rates move over time, the naira value you receive at withdrawal may be different — higher or lower — than the naira amount you originally deposited.

This is not a guaranteed-return product. Its value depends on movements in the exchange rate between the time of deposit and the time of withdrawal.

How Other Products Work

The Investment Savings Account, Emergency Savings Account, and 100 Days Savings Challenge operate on a straightforward record-keeping basis: your contributions are recorded by SAVEMORE in naira, and your balance grows as you contribute, according to the minimum amounts, lock-up periods, and durations of each product (see our Fees & Charges page for specific figures).

Transparency Through the Member Portal

Every confirmed transaction is recorded and visible to you on your Member Portal dashboard, including the date, product, description, amount, and your running balance. You can also download a receipt for confirmed transactions.
