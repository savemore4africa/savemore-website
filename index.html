MORE 1.0

What is MORE?

MORE is the proprietary participation unit of SAVEMORE. MORE represents a participant's recognized participation interest within the SAVEMORE ecosystem and provides defined rights under the MORE framework.


The total supply of MORE is fixed at 200 MORE, with each unit carrying equal participation and dividend rights.

1 MORE = 1 SAVEMORE Participation Unit

MORE is not the Nigerian Naira, a foreign currency, eNaira, USDT, or any other conventional currency.


Read the full MORE explanation

The economic reference value of MORE is determined by the SAVEMORE participation formula:

MORE Reference Value = ₦5n

where n represents the total number of paid SAVEMORE applicants.


For example, if there were 40 paid applicants, the reference value of one MORE would be:

₦5 × 40 = ₦200

The reference value changes as the number of paid applicants changes.


The circulating MORE units are supported by the naira value designated by SAVEMORE from members' commitment fees paid in connection with savings services rendered. Such backing shall be separately recorded and accounted for by SAVEMORE.


The supply of MORE is fixed at 200 MORE. No additional MORE will be created beyond this supply. MORE that is sold back to SAVEMORE may be issued again. MORE is issued by SAVEMORE in accordance with its approved participation, issuance, accounting and governance rules, subject to applicable laws and regulations.


MORE is therefore created at the conceptual/system level by SAVEMORE's establishment of the MORE framework, issued by SAVEMORE according to its participation rules, and supported economically by the designated backing associated with the circulating units.


MORE shall not be represented as legal tender or as a sovereign currency. Any future transferability, redemption, exchangeability, blockchain tokenisation, merchant use, or public offering shall be subject to the applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and Shariah requirements.


The full MORE 1.0: Rights, Issuance & Dividend Framework document explains how MORE is acquired, its progressive commitment-fee structure, dividend rights, transfer and sell-back rules, and the protections in place for MORE holders.
