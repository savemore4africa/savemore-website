Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, how SAVEMORE collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of our members. We want you to understand exactly what happens to your information.

1. Information We Collect

Name and Contact Information

Your full name, phone number, email address, and residential/shop addresses.

KYC Information

Your date of birth, occupation, National Identification Number (NIN), bank name, account number, account name, and next of kin details, which we collect to verify your identity and maintain accurate membership records.

Transaction Information

Records of your deposits, withdrawals, and any adjustments made to your savings accounts, along with dates and reference numbers.

Savings Records

Your chosen savings product(s), balances, participation (MORE) units, and savings goals.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your member account and Member ID.
  • To verify your identity as part of our KYC process.
  • To record and track your savings contributions and withdrawals.
  • To communicate with you about your account, including notifications when your account is updated.
  • To issue certificates and manage MORE participation records.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Member information is stored electronically using Firebase, a cloud database and authentication service provided by Google. Access to member records is restricted: you can only view your own information, and only authorized SAVEMORE administrators can view or update member records generally.

4. Who Information May Be Shared With

We do not sell member information. Information may be shared with:

  • SAVEMORE administrators and staff, for the purpose of managing your account.
  • Service providers that help SAVEMORE operate the website and Member Portal (such as our hosting and database providers), strictly to the extent needed to provide those services.
  • Regulatory or law enforcement authorities, where required by applicable Nigerian law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including restricting database access through authentication and access-control rules, and masking sensitive details such as your NIN and account number by default on your dashboard. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Member Rights

You have the right to know what information SAVEMORE holds about you, which you can view directly on your Member Portal dashboard. If you believe any information is inaccurate, please contact SAVEMORE to request a correction.

7. Data Retention

We retain member information for as long as your membership is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to maintain accurate records, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website and Member Portal do not use tracking cookies or advertising technologies. The site relies on secure, authenticated sessions to identify logged-in members rather than browser cookies for tracking purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

SAVEMORE uses the following third-party services to operate its Member Portal and communications:

  • Firebase (Google) — for secure login and storing member data.
  • EmailJS — for sending account-related email notifications.

These providers process information solely to deliver the services described, and are not authorized to use member information for their own purposes.

10. Nigerian Context

SAVEMORE is a Nigerian business operating under Moresales Venture. We handle member information responsibly and in line with general data protection principles. This policy does not claim certification under any specific data protection regulation or licence.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices evolve. Material changes will be communicated to members. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
