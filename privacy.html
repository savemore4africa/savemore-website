Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, how SAVEMORE collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of our members. We want you to understand exactly what happens to your information.

1. Information We Collect

Name and Contact Information

Your full name, phone number, email address, and residential/shop addresses.

KYC Information

Your date of birth, occupation, National Identification Number (NIN), bank name, account number, account name, and next of kin details, which we collect to verify your identity and maintain accurate membership records. If someone referred you, we also record the Member ID of that person.

Transaction Information

Records of your deposits, withdrawals, and any adjustments made to your savings accounts, along with dates and reference numbers.

Savings Records

Your chosen savings product(s), balances, participation (MORE) units, and savings goals.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your member account and Member ID.
  • To verify your identity as part of our KYC process.
  • To record and track your savings contributions and withdrawals.
  • To communicate with you about your account, including notifications when your account is updated.
  • To issue certificates and manage MORE participation records.
  • To process your payment confirmations, withdrawal requests and MORE sell-back requests.
  • To notify our administrators by email when you register or when important account events happen.
  • To keep accurate records, help prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations.

We process your information with your consent, to provide the services you request, and where we need to do so to meet our legal obligations.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

Member information is stored electronically using Firebase, a cloud database and authentication service provided by Google. Access to member records is restricted: you can only view your own information, and only authorized SAVEMORE administrators can view or update member records generally.

The servers of our database provider may be located outside Nigeria, so your information may be processed outside Nigeria. We choose established providers and take reasonable steps to keep your information protected.

4. Who Information May Be Shared With

We do not sell member information. Information may be shared with:

  • SAVEMORE administrators and staff, for the purpose of managing your account.
  • Service providers that help SAVEMORE operate the website and Member Portal (such as our hosting and database providers), strictly to the extent needed to provide those services.
  • Regulatory or law enforcement authorities, where required by applicable Nigerian law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including restricting database access through authentication and access-control rules, and masking sensitive details such as your NIN and account number by default on your dashboard. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please keep your password and transaction PIN private, and never share them with anyone, including people who claim to represent SAVEMORE.

If your information is affected by a data breach: where the law requires it, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, and we will inform affected members if the breach is likely to put their rights and freedoms at risk.

6. Member Rights

Under Nigerian data protection law, you have rights over your personal information. These include the right to:

  • know what information SAVEMORE holds about you. You can view most of it directly on your Member Portal dashboard;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, although we may need to keep some records while you are an active member or where the law requires us to;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your information;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which will not affect what we did before you withdrew it;
  • ask for a copy of your information in a commonly used format;
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe your information has been mishandled.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 12. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Data Retention

We retain member information for as long as your membership is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to maintain accurate records, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we will delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website and Member Portal do not use tracking cookies or advertising technologies. The site relies on secure, authenticated sessions to identify logged-in members rather than browser cookies for tracking purposes.

9. Third-Party Services

SAVEMORE uses the following third-party services to operate its Member Portal and communications:

  • Firebase (Google) — for secure login and storing member data.
  • EmailJS — for sending account-related email notifications, including emails to SAVEMORE administrators when a new member registers. These emails contain the member's Member ID, name, phone number, email address and chosen product.
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting the SAVEMORE website.

These providers process information solely to deliver the services described, and are not authorized to use member information for their own purposes.

10. Nigerian Context

SAVEMORE is a Nigerian business operating under Moresales Venture. We handle member information responsibly and aim to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and its principles. This policy does not claim certification under any specific data protection scheme or licence.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices evolve. Material changes will be communicated to members. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
