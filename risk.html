Risk Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

SAVEMORE believes members should make informed decisions. This page explains, plainly and without exaggeration, the risks associated with SAVEMORE's products so you can decide what is right for you.

1. Products Have Different Terms

SAVEMORE's four savings products — Investment Savings, Emergency Savings, In-Dollar Savings, and the 100 Days Savings Challenge — each have different minimum amounts, lock-up periods, and durations. Please read the specific terms of a product on our Terms & Conditions page before participating.

2. Currency and Exchange-Rate Risk

The In-Dollar Savings Account involves exchange-rate risk. Deposits are used to acquire USDT at the time of deposit, and this USDT is sold at the applicable rate at withdrawal. If the exchange rate moves unfavourably between your deposit and withdrawal dates, the naira value you receive could be lower than what you originally deposited. See our How Your Money Is Handled page for a full explanation of this mechanism.

3. Past Performance Is Not a Guarantee

Any past growth, value, or outcome associated with SAVEMORE's products or the MORE participation framework is not a guarantee or prediction of future results.

4. MORE's Reference Value Is Not Guaranteed Cash Value

MORE's reference value, calculated using SAVEMORE's ₦5n formula, is an internal calculated value used within SAVEMORE's participation framework. It should not be treated as guaranteed cash you can withdraw on demand, unless SAVEMORE's actual governing rules for MORE establish that at a given time. Any future transferability, sell-back, or redemption of MORE is subject to SAVEMORE's applicable rules and conditions.

5. No Guaranteed Dividends

MORE CAPITAL, where applicable, may generate legitimate investment or business profit from which dividends could potentially be distributed to MORE holders. However, no dividend amount is promised or guaranteed. Any distribution depends on actual profit performance and SAVEMORE's dividend framework at the relevant time.

6. Understand Before You Commit

Members should take time to understand the terms of a savings product — including its minimum contribution, lock-up period, and any applicable fees — before committing funds. If anything is unclear, please contact SAVEMORE before proceeding.

7. Only Commit What You Can Set Aside

Members should not commit money they cannot afford to set aside for the duration required by the applicable product terms, particularly for products with lock-up periods. Saving should support your financial wellbeing, not create financial strain.

8. General Business Risk

As with any savings or financial participation arrangement, there is a general risk associated with the performance and operations of the business itself. SAVEMORE seeks to operate transparently and responsibly, maintaining proper records and communicating material changes to members.

Questions about any of these risks are welcome. Please reach out to us on WhatsApp at 08031509452 before making a decision you're unsure about.
