SAVEMORE operates four distinct savings products, each with its own specific features. This page sets out the general terms that apply across SAVEMORE. Members should also read the specific terms applicable to the particular savings product(s) they choose before participating.

1. Introduction

These Terms & Conditions govern your use of SAVEMORE's website, Member Portal, and savings products. SAVEMORE is a savings and financial support platform operated under Moresales Venture. By registering as a member or using SAVEMORE's services, you agree to these terms.

2. Eligibility

To become a SAVEMORE member, an applicant must be able to provide accurate personal information, valid means of identification, and complete the applicable registration and KYC requirements. SAVEMORE may decline or suspend an application where information provided cannot be verified.

3. Account Registration

Registration requires providing your full name, phone number, email address, date of birth, residential address, occupation, bank details, NIN, and next of kin information, and selecting a savings product. A unique Member ID is issued upon successful registration. Members are responsible for keeping their login details confidential.

4. KYC Requirements

"Know Your Customer" (KYC) information is collected to verify member identity and maintain proper records. This includes valid identification, NIN, bank account details, and next of kin information. SAVEMORE reviews this information before fully activating a membership. See our KYC page for the current list of requirements.

5. Savings Accounts (General)

SAVEMORE currently offers four savings products: the Investment Savings Account, the Emergency Savings Account, the In-Dollar Savings Account, and the 100 Days Savings Challenge. Each product has its own minimum contribution, lock-up period, and maximum duration, as set out below and on the SAVEMORE website.

6. Deposits

Members make contributions according to the rules of their chosen savings product. Deposits are recorded by SAVEMORE and reflected in the member's account on the Member Portal. Members are responsible for ensuring deposit amounts meet the applicable minimum for their chosen product.

7. Withdrawals

Withdrawals are subject to the lock-up period and other conditions of the applicable savings product. Requests are reviewed and processed by SAVEMORE. Specific withdrawal timelines and any applicable conditions will be communicated according to the applicable product terms.

8. Fees and Charges

SAVEMORE applies a progressive Application/Renewal (Commitment) Fee based on the total number of applicants at the time of application or renewal. Full current figures are published on our Fees & Charges page. Any other fee not currently defined will be communicated according to the applicable product terms before it takes effect.

9. Account Records

SAVEMORE maintains records of member information, contributions, withdrawals, and participation history. Members can view their own transaction history and savings information through the Member Portal.

10. 100 Days Savings Challenge

This product is structured around 20 progressive slots across five-day contribution cycles (20 × 5 days = 100 days), with a minimum deposit of ₦500. It is designed to encourage consistent daily or periodic saving over a 100-day period.

11. In-Dollar Savings Account

Deposits into the In-Dollar Savings Account are used by SAVEMORE to acquire USDT at the applicable rate at the time of deposit. At withdrawal, the USDT is sold at the applicable rate at that time. This means the naira value a member receives at withdrawal may differ from the naira amount originally deposited, due to exchange-rate movement. The minimum deposit is ₦5,000, with a 10-week lock-up period. See our How Your Money Is Handled page for a fuller explanation.

12. Investment Savings Account

This account has a minimum saving amount of ₦1,000, a minimum lock-up duration of 10 weeks, and a maximum savings duration of 50 weeks. It is designed around disciplined savings rather than interest-bearing deposits.

13. Emergency Savings Account

This account has a minimum saving amount of ₦500, no lock-up period, and a maximum savings duration of 50 weeks, providing more immediate access for emergency-oriented savings needs.

14. MORE Participation Framework

MORE is SAVEMORE's proprietary participation unit, described fully on our MORE page and in the MORE 1.0 document. MORE is not Nigerian naira or any other currency. Its reference value is calculated using SAVEMORE's formula and may change over time. Holding MORE does not guarantee any specific cash value, dividend, or return unless expressly stated in SAVEMORE's governing MORE documentation.
